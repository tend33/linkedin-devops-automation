{% if kind == 'Privacy notice' %}

The deployment stores supplied topics, AI-generated drafts and images, topic history, research sources, the authenticated member identifier, token expiry and scopes, approval records and publication receipts. Your lite profile is fetched once at OAuth connection to obtain the member identifier; other returned name/photo fields are discarded. Connections, comments, likes and feeds are not collected. Tokens are encrypted at rest with a deployment-specific key. Successful login sessions last 30 minutes; unsuccessful login counters use source IP addresses and reset every 15 minutes.

Topics and generation prompts are sent to the configured AI provider (OpenAI by default), which may also perform public-web research. Do not enter confidential or third-party personal information. Credentials and your LinkedIn member identifier are not sent to the AI provider. After explicit approval, saved text and the optional image are sent to LinkedIn for public publication. Hosting operators with filesystem or Docker access can access local data.

Withdraw consent using Disconnect: it deletes the local OAuth token and pending approvals and blocks fallback credentials. Revoke the application's access in LinkedIn settings too. An already submitted publication may complete; published posts remain on LinkedIn until you remove them there.

As the sole owner/operator, you can delete draft JSON/text, images, topic-history.txt, logs, jenkins-builds and publication records directly from the deployment data directory, and delete Jenkins artifacts and backups according to your retention policy. Before deleting publication receipts or locks, stop publishing and reconcile LinkedIn status to avoid duplicate posts. Identity and expired encrypted tokens remain until disconnected or deleted. Drafts and publication receipts remain until you delete them. The operator must control backup retention and securely delete copies. Before providing access to other users, replace this notice with accurate operator contact, retention and deletion-request details and implement individual accounts.
