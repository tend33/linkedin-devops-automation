Connect your LinkedIn account

This single-owner app fetches your lite profile once when you connect and retains only the member identifier (name/photo fields are discarded) and stores an encrypted publishing token until you disconnect or delete it. It sends only the saved post text and optional image you explicitly approve to LinkedIn for public publication. Draft generation sends your supplied topics to the configured AI provider; optional research uses that provider's web-search service. LinkedIn credentials and identity are not sent to the AI provider.

You can withdraw consent and delete the local token and pending approvals using Disconnect on the dashboard. Remove the app in LinkedIn settings to revoke authorization there. Disconnect does not remove posts already published. To delete drafts, publication logs, backups or published posts, follow the privacy notice. Credentials expiring requires reconnecting.
